## Originality checks
- **Andy Woodhead**: 164 own models (historical sculpts, 3D-print figures, the "rigged for UE4/5" series). This is the same author the hero
  prototype already accepted.
- **Johnpaul.boyle**: a helmet series (Sallet, Gladiator, Griffin Sallet, Norman) plus other props. A same-name "Norman Helmet" by MetaProx
  is a different model.
- **Mister-Smash**: a crown series (simple iron, medium bronze/silver, rich gold) plus weapons.
- **JeanStroh**: a bow and arrow series (Base/Compound/Recurve bow and arrows).
- **Gammawolf**: a weapon and helmet series. The other "Spartan Spear" uploads are different models by other people.
- **Mu.Mu1 (Alethea): medium risk.** This uploader has only 2 models (Alethea, Perin Dragon). The part names are Vietnamese
  (Vay = skirt, Kiem = sword, Day_chuyen = necklace), which suggests the uploader authored it. I found no same-name upload by anyone else.
  If the jury needs zero doubt, keep Shamiram off-screen or use her only at a distance.
